# AI skills

Reusable skills organized by the tool they were developed with. Each skill folder includes installation instructions and its reuse terms.

## ChatGPT

- [Berkshire Hathaway Business Analysis](ChatGPT/skills/berkshire-business-analysis) Apply the shareholder letters’ lessons about owner earnings, durable businesses, capital allocation, and valuation.
- [Howard Marks Research](ChatGPT/skills/howard-marks-research) Apply Oaktree’s memos to price, risk, credit, cycles, and potential permanent loss.
- [Mauboussin Research](ChatGPT/skills/mauboussin-research) applies the author's research to business analysis, competitive advantage, capital allocation, and expectations investing. It produces a 1–100 investment score, a five-star rating, and a one-page executive summary.

## Claude

- [Mauboussin Research](Claude/skills/mauboussin-research) is the Claude edition of the Mauboussin Research skill. It answers and cites questions across the 183-paper Research archive, analyzes a business as an investment (1–100 score, five-star rating, one-page executive summary), and keeps the library current with monthly intake. Install it in Claude Code from the folder or upload [mauboussin-research.zip](Claude/skills/mauboussin-research.zip) in the Claude apps.
