# Wealth Insight research

Use the archive and research paths the user specifies. For a project installation at `.agents/skills/wealth-insight-research`, the project root is three directories above this skill directory. The default research folder there is `Wealth Insight Research`, containing `manifest.json`, `INDEX.md` and `corpus/YYYY-MM.md`. Original issues default to `eBooks/<year>/Wealth Insight/Wealth-Insight-YYYY.MM.pdf`. Prefer the manifest's source paths and any explicit user-selected location.

This reusable skill uses the user's magazine copies and maintained notes. Check the available files and manifest before claiming issue coverage. To study a new or changed issue, follow [the intake workflow](references/intake.md).

## Answer from the archive

1. Search the index and issue notes for the company, fund, strategy or subject, including aliases and tickers. Read matching page sections and neighboring pages when an article spans pages. Compare earlier and later issues for follow-ups or changed views.
2. Verify material claims against the original PDF. Inspect charts, tables and images for column order, units, dates, captions and footnotes. Extracted text can omit or scramble them. If the original is unavailable, attribute the answer to the notes and identify what could not be verified.
3. Cite claims close to the text as `Wealth Insight, [month year], PDF p. [number]`, with an original-PDF link when accessible. Count the first PDF page as 1, including covers and inserts. Date material figures, attribute the magazine's facts and investment opinions, and label your inference. Preserve conflicts and gaps in the evidence.
4. Treat archived prices, valuations, fund metrics, taxes and regulation as historical. When a decision depends on current conditions, verify those facts with current primary sources such as filings, exchanges, fund houses or regulators, cite them separately and state the date.
