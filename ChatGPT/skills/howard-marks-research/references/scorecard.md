# Investment score and executive summary

The score expresses the analyst’s judgment about owning a particular security at a particular price. Howard Marks did not publish these weights or star thresholds. A high score means stronger prospective compensation for the risks taken. It does not measure the probability of a gain.

| Factor | Points available | Evidence that earns points |
|---|---:|---|
| Earning power | 20 | Explain how customers create cash earnings, who captures the economics, how much capital is required and how durable that claim is. Normalize unusual income. |
| Protection against permanent loss | 25 | Trace permanent impairment, funding needs and the ability to endure adversity. Inspect claim priority, covenants, liquidity and customer or regulatory concentration where relevant. |
| Price versus conservative value | 25 | Tie the price to a range of cash flows or normalized earnings. Show a conservative central case, the required return or an investable alternative, and what must be true to justify the quote. |
| Cycle and expectations | 15 | Identify observable changes in credit, competition, investor behavior or industry conditions. Establish what expectations the price appears to reflect, and distinguish an inferred hurdle from observed consensus. |
| Payoff asymmetry | 15 | Compare plausible outcomes in the same units over the same horizon. Account for the magnitude of loss, upside foregone and the robustness of the thesis to a mistaken assumption. |

Award integer points up to each ceiling. Rough anchors per factor are zero for evidence of a severe weakness, half for a mixed case, and full credit for a demonstrable strength that survives a demanding test. Explain departures from the midpoint. Keep each fact attached to the question it answers. For example, a liability schedule belongs under loss protection, the central case belongs under price, and sensitivity to alternatives belongs under asymmetry. Weak evidence warrants lower confidence and explicit conditional scores when it could reverse the conclusion. If a contemporaneous price cannot be verified, state a dated reference price or show explicitly conditional ratings at assumed prices.

Add the five earned amounts, with a floor of 1 and a ceiling of 100. Map the total only once. Always print both the numeral and the visual stars.

## The one-page brief

Lead with the security, date, reference price, score, visual stars and an actionable research conclusion. Explain the business in one sentence. Tell the reader which advantage could persist, where capital can be lost, and how much margin for error the price provides. A small scenario table can carry the horizon, assumptions and comparable returns. Close with the strongest contrary evidence and what observation or price changes the assessment. Keep calculations reproducible in the body or supporting analysis and link the decisive primary sources.

For future dated examples, record the factors and total alongside the summary. Use currency, taxation and distribution assumptions that apply to the case. Preserve historical analyses as dated snapshots.
