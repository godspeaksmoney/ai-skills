## Executive summary

IEX earns fees when electricity trades on its platform. Its deep order book has attracted both buyers and sellers, and its bidding and settlement software is integrated into customer workflows. The company reported 39.7 billion units traded in the September quarter, up 12.7% year on year. Consolidated profit after tax reached ₹139.24 crore. Management estimated its share of each of the day-ahead and real-time markets at over 99%. [IEX Q2 filing](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/ixbrl/INTEGRATED_FILING_INDAS_206465_29102026173347_iXBRL_WEB.html) · [Earnings call, pp. 5, 15–17](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/IEX_05112026110840_IEX_Intimation_EarningCall_Transcript_Q2_Sep26Signed.pdf)

CERC has proposed pooling bids from competing exchanges at Grid India. A utility could then submit its order elsewhere and receive the same clearing price in an unconstrained market. IEX would retain its customer interface, and changing established systems costs customers time. The critical uncertainty is how many orders it keeps, and at what fee, once access to the shared order book is available to rivals. I found the amendment in the draft register and no final notification or segment start date in the current register as of this assessment. [CERC draft, §§3–4](https://cercind.gov.in/2026/draft_reg/DN-PM-2ndAmd.pdf) · [Draft register](https://www.cercind.gov.in/Draft_reg.html) · [Current regulations](https://cercind.gov.in/current_reg.html)

At ₹113.15 the stock trades at 19.3 times trailing diluted earnings of ₹5.87. With year-three EPS of ₹7.10, an 18× exit multiple and ₹10 of aggregate dividends, the illustrative annualized return is 6.8%. The same assumptions support a 12% annual return at roughly ₹98 today. At the quoted price, the same hurdle requires year-three EPS of ₹8.28, or about 12.1% annual EPS growth. A profitable exchange can retain most customers and still deliver an ordinary shareholder return. [NSE closing file, IEX EQ](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/cm/BhavCopy_NSE_CM_0_0_0_20261120_F_0000.csv.zip) · [FY26 filing](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/ixbrl/INTEGRATED_FILING_INDAS_152396_23042026181158_iXBRL_WEB.html) · [H1 FY26 filing](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/ixbrl/INTEGRATED_FILING_INDAS_123488_30102025171301_iXBRL_WEB.html) · [H1 FY27 filing](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/ixbrl/INTEGRATED_FILING_INDAS_206465_29102026173347_iXBRL_WEB.html)

**Decision.** Keep IEX on the watchlist. A lower quote around ₹98 would warrant re-underwriting the central case. A final coupling procedure, followed by two quarters of segment share and fee retention, would give better grounds for revising the economics. A loss of high-volume customers or fee concessions would weaken the case even if total Indian exchange volumes continued to grow.
