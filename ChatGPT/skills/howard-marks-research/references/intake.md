# Monthly intake

The public archive is organized by publication year. Scope begins in 1990 and extends through the date of the run. Some older individual links open PDFs through JavaScript. The dated October 2025 introduction to an anthology has its own memo page; its signed cover letter is the in-scope item. Skip the Compilations tab and its downloads.

1. Retrieve the [publisher index](https://www.oaktreecapital.com/insights/memos). The standard-library helper `python scripts/inventory.py --as-of YYYY-MM-DD` emits candidate JSON to stdout. It also accepts `--html path/to/index.html` for a saved copy. Compare candidates to `inventory.json`, including earlier years. On this project run the helper from the skill folder, or pass its full path. A browser or another HTTP client can retrieve a fresh index for offline parsing when necessary.
2. Match date and displayed title first, then compare direct URLs. Reconcile renamed pages by reading both entries or identifying the same publication. A changed PDF version or publisher correction merits comparison. A missing older row calls for investigation. Preserve its historical study. If layout changes, compare the visible year headings and counts and adapt the parser before accepting its output.
3. Read each new or changed individual memo through the end, including substantive appendices, exhibits or footnotes. For dated anniversary letters use the signed original text on their dated page. If extraction omits a substantive page, render or OCR it. Distinguish entries with similar titles, guest material and coauthors. Keep the date printed by the archive and explain any material difference inside the document.
4. Add original notes to the appropriate period file. Record the displayed date, title, direct source, central claim, a concrete example or mechanism, and a decision or boundary condition. Revisit the framework where a new memo extends, qualifies or contradicts a previous lesson. Preserve historical calls with their original dates. A monthly memo update does not require refreshing the IEX example.
5. Check the one-to-one correspondence between dated entries and notes. Publish the verified notes and any framework changes before replacing the inventory and coverage counts. Retry a failed link at its individual Oaktree location. Record remaining gaps with title, date, URL and reason in coverage, and revisit them next run. A failed fetch is distinct from a successful check with no additions. Rerunning the same edition should produce no duplicate notes.
6. Report the latest memo date, additions, meaningful revisions and unresolved entries. When no substantive change is found, retain the existing studies.

From the skill folder, `python scripts/inventory.py --as-of YYYY-MM-DD --compare references/inventory.json` reports additions, changed links and entries missing from the current listing. Inspect a possible rename before treating it as a new memo. In the validated candidate folder, `python scripts/verify_notes.py` checks each dated note against its inventory title and direct URL, including equivalent URL encodings. A clean comparison covers index changes; detecting silent edits to a body under the same URL requires checking the individual publication.
