# Charlie Munger’s Almanack

A Codex skill for applying *Poor Charlie’s Almanack* to hard decisions. It helps you find who benefits from a proposal, work backward from failure, distinguish a good business from a good price, and identify the observation that would change your mind. You can also ask a question about the book and trace the answer to the chapter or talk.

## Install in a Codex project

Download or clone the repository. Copy the entire `ChatGPT/skills/charlie-munger-almanack` folder into your project’s `.agents/skills/` directory. Open the project in a new Codex chat and invoke `$charlie-munger-almanack`.

## Try it

- `Use $charlie-munger-almanack to evaluate this factory upgrade. Our competitors can buy the same machine. What determines whether we keep the savings?`
- `Use $charlie-munger-almanack to review our hiring decision. Everyone endorsed the founder’s preferred candidate. How much independent evidence do we have?`
- `Use $charlie-munger-almanack to explain how the outcome of the USAir investment could make our assessment of the original decision worse.`

The [source guide](references/source-guide.md) maps all eleven talks and the surrounding material. See the [investment guide](references/investment.md) for a worked capital-allocation example and the [misjudgment guide](references/misjudgment.md) for an example of several incentives and biases reinforcing one another.
