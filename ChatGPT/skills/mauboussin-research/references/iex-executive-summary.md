Historical worked example using public sources.

## Investment view

IEX earns high margins and generates substantial cash from its electricity trading platform. At ₹115.17, the share price requires stronger growth or better fee and share retention than the base case supports. The probability-weighted estimate is **₹91 per share**, about **21% below** that close. [19 Nov NSE close](https://trackliveprice.com/stocks/iex/)

## What drives the result

- **Highly profitable platform.** FY26 electricity volume rose 17% to 141.1 billion units. Consolidated operating revenue was ₹616 crore, net profit was ₹493 crore, and operating cash flow less cash capex was about ₹418 crore. In Q2 FY27, volume grew 12.7% to 39.7 billion units and consolidated net profit grew 12.9% to ₹139 crore. [FY26 annual report](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/annual_reports/AR_30351_IEX_2025_2026_A_25828450_14082026160534.pdf) · [Q2 FY27 filings](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/IEX_29102026170658_IEX_Intimation_Investor_presentation_Q2_Sep26_signed.pdf)
- **Coupling could weaken IEX’s liquidity advantage.** CERC’s draft would have Grid India pool bids and determine clearing prices across exchanges. IEX’s customer integrations and settlement workflows could retain volume. Common pricing could make it easier for customers to switch or trade on multiple venues, putting fees and share under pressure. Day-ahead and real-time accounted for about 83% of IEX’s FY26 electricity volume combined. CERC still listed the amendment as a draft at review. [CERC draft](https://cercind.gov.in/2026/draft_reg/DN-PM-2ndAmd.pdf) · [CERC current regulations](https://cercind.gov.in/current_reg.html) · [FY26 annual report](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/annual_reports/AR_30351_IEX_2025_2026_A_25828450_14082026160534.pdf)

At the quoted price, holding the base case’s share and fee paths, discount rate and terminal growth constant, the model requires **16.6% annual growth in the addressable exchange volume for five years**, versus roughly 11% in the base case. The scenario weights are analyst judgments.

**Reassess when** CERC publishes final rules and segment start dates, when post-coupling market share and fees become observable, or when the Indian Gas Exchange divestment has firm terms.
