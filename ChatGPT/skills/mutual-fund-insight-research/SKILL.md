# Mutual Fund Insight research

Use the user's authorized magazine archive and maintained research library. Locate its manifest, topic index, cross-issue synthesis, page-level notes, review audits and original PDFs. Preserve the established layout. If these are unavailable, identify what is missing; the public package does not contain the detailed originating archive.

Read the [research principles](references/research-principles.md) for the reusable methods. The published coverage record describes the originating study; it does not establish access to that evidence in another installation.

## Answer from the research

1. Search the index, knowledge guide and issue notes for the question, fund aliases, category, plan/option and period. Read complete matching page sections and neighboring pages of the article. Compare later/earlier issues where views or figures change.
2. Verify material numerical claims against original PDF pages, especially tables/charts. Inspect labels, units, dates, regular/direct plan, growth/IDCW option, annualised versus absolute return, benchmark, sample selection and footnotes. Use rendered images for scrambled columns, missing image text and corrupted fonts. Reviewed notes preserve known errors; never let a raw text match override a recorded caveat.
3. Cite claims close to the text as `Mutual Fund Insight, [month year], PDF p. [one-based position]`, with a link to the original PDF when accessible. PDF positions include covers, inserts and supplements; printed folios can differ. If you cannot verify a source page, attribute to the notes and say so.
4. Separate the magazine's reported facts, interviewee opinions, advertisements and your inference. Missing history is not a poor rating; percentages of rated assets are not percentages of schemes. Preserve any disagreement rather than silently choosing a figure. Common problems include anomalous ETF return tables, different investor-XIRR/fund-return dates, stale footnotes and manager forecasts posed as facts. Consult the knowledge guide and issue audit for specifics.
5. Treat archived NAVs, returns, fund access, costs, regulation and tax/succession statements as historical. For a decision needing current facts, check current primary sources such as fund disclosures, AMFI, SEBI, RBI and tax authorities, cite them separately and state the date. If current sources are unavailable, identify what remains unverified. Do not imply the hosted agent has capabilities its saved configuration lacks.
6. Explain goals, horizons, allocation, risk, costs and behavioral tradeoffs with evidence. For personal scenarios ask only for materially missing circumstances. Do not turn a dated league table or promotion into an unconditional buy/sell instruction, assume all goals use the same horizon, or promise SIPs prevent losses.
