# Research principles

These principles guide evidence gathering. They do not reproduce the private reading notes or establish current fund terms. Retrieve the user's original issue before making an issue-specific claim.

## Cash flows and SIPs

Distinguish investor cash-flow XIRR from a fund's time-weighted or trailing return. Identify contribution dates, valuation date, plan, option, benchmark and annualisation. A young SIP can have a volatile XIRR; a step-up changes contributed capital as well as the final corpus. A historical contribution-to-corpus crossover can reverse. SIPs do not guarantee a profit or a fixed recovery period.

## Allocation and active management

Compare net directional exposure, hedges, credit, commodities, costs and portfolio role within a category. Gross equity exposure and net market exposure answer different questions. Category names and marketing phrases do not establish capital protection.

Assess fund size relative to opportunity-set liquidity, free float, ownership and liquidation capacity. Active share measures differentiation from a benchmark; it does not establish skill. Check survivorship, comparison groups, fees and whether the selected benchmark matches the portfolio.

## Factors and passive tools

Separate backtested index history from live performance. Inspect factor definitions, concentration, turnover, rebalancing and sponsor incentives. Rolling-return dispersion and daily volatility are different measures; correlations of excess returns and total returns are also different.

For passive products consider tracking difference, expenses, spreads, liquidity, premiums to NAV, index construction and portfolio overlap. A displayed expense ratio does not capture every implementation cost.

## Young funds and complex products

Missing history is unavailable evidence. Preliminary screens and short peer rankings are not a long-history rating. For newer or derivative-based products inspect actual scheme rules, eligibility, permitted exposures and loss mechanisms. Novelty or prestige does not establish suitability.

## Retirement and international access

Separate a dated goal from spending over decades. Deduct commitments already promised to other goals before judging retirement resources. Distinguish shortfall models, historical withdrawal scenarios and ruin-probability analysis. State inflation, cash-flow, longevity, sequence and tax assumptions; a selected successful backtest does not establish a universal safe withdrawal rate.

For overseas exposure distinguish economic diversification from the access route. Check current subscription limits, premiums, eligibility, costs and reporting requirements against primary sources before action.

## Evidence and numerical inconsistencies

Record dates, units, plan/option, sample, benchmark and footnotes together. Fund-house asset percentages can have different denominators from scheme counts. Interview forecasts, advertisements and editorial analysis have different evidential weight.

If a table conflicts with prose, another issue, an investment-value column or its own footnote, preserve the disagreement and inspect the original image. Do not infer a corrected value merely because one figure looks more plausible. Extracted rating glyphs, colored dots and chart columns can lose their meaning.

Cite one-based PDF positions including covers, inserts and supplements. Retain unreadable items and unresolved inconsistencies in the private review audit. Confirm changing financial, legal and product facts using current primary sources when a decision depends on them.
