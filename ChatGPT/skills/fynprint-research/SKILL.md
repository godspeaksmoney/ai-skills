# The Fynprint research

Use the user's own authorized magazine archive and original reading notes. This skill provides a research method; it does not contain magazine issues, a studied archive, or access to another person's private research agent.

## Find the evidence

Locate the archive and any user-designated reading notes in the current project. An example layout is `eBooks/<year>/The fynprint/The Fynprint Volume NN.pdf`; preserve an established convention. If the sources are not available, ask for their location or the relevant issue. Inspect actual files before stating which editions have been reviewed.

Search notes for candidate issues, then inspect the original PDF pages supporting material claims. The cover is PDF page 1; distinguish PDF page numbers from printed page labels. Include the volume, issue date or range, and PDF page citation. Link the original file when the interface supports it. If only notes are available, identify that limitation.

## Answer and compare

Resolve claims at the level of the actual product, variant, wrapper and reporting date. Inspect chart images, captions, column headings, footnotes and proposal status. Separate interviews, editorial views, advertisements, estimates and independently corroborated facts.

For fund or route comparisons, align currency, share class, inception and measurement dates, absolute versus annualized returns, pre-tax versus post-tax results, fee layers and the correct benchmark. Separate platform, instrument domicile, underlying assets, investor residence, and investor-level versus fund-level tax. Preserve unresolved discrepancies rather than silently choosing one number.

For current fees, eligibility, prices, regulations or tax rules, verify relevant primary sources such as the issuer, RBI, SEBI, IFSCA, PFRDA, IRDAI or Income Tax Department. Attribute dated magazine reporting separately from current verification. Explain material assumptions and evidence gaps.

Ground the answer in the pages actually checked. A concise answer should make the conclusion, evidence, reporting date and any important unresolved point clear. Avoid turning a manager's target, historical return or interview opinion into an assured investment outcome.

## Study a new issue

Read [the intake procedure](references/intake.md) when reviewing new issues or maintaining an authorized private search collection. Review every page, including image-only pages, tables and advertising sections. Use rendering and OCR as needed, and visually verify material labels and figures. Save original page-cited notes and a durable review record before attempting any downstream update.

Treat local review, skill coverage and hosted synchronization as separate states. Report a complete intake only after all requested destinations have been verified. Resume pending work even when its issue already appears in the notes.

If maintaining an authorized public workflow edition, also follow the intake procedure's publication check after every intake and whenever these instructions change. Preserve a private receipt of the reviewed public version, including a verified no-change result when appropriate.
