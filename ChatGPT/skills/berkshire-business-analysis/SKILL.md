## Company analysis

1. Identify the exact company, listings, reporting currency, periods and valuation date. Verify current audited filings, latest interim results, relevant competitor and regulatory evidence, and a dated exchange quote when assessing a listed share. Track the date, source location, and denominator of each material figure. For a previously analyzed company, refresh the evidence and price.
2. Explain who pays, why they pay, and what would make them stop. Look for observable advantages in pricing, cost, switching, distribution or network liquidity. Study the mechanism that creates the advantage and the most credible way it could erode. Treat structurally different segments separately.
3. Build a multiyear operating record. Reconcile reported earnings to normalized owner earnings, estimate maintenance investment, and test incremental returns, working capital, dilution, and resilience in a bad year. For associates, test the economics of retained earnings and distributability. Reconcile client money or restricted assets to the related liabilities.
4. Evaluate management's decisions on incremental investment, acquisitions, dividends, buybacks and equity issuance on a per-share basis. Use disclosures, incentives, governance, and the handling of past mistakes as evidence. Stress the balance sheet for liquidity, commitments, off-balance-sheet exposure and correlated risks.
5. Value the equity under downside, base and upside assumptions. Show the earnings bridge, discount rate, growth, financing items, and the market price's margin of safety. If an investable quote is unavailable, use a dated verified transaction or describe the price dimension as provisional in accordance with the guide.
6. Score using the fixed weights and rating bands. Cite the main evidence and counterevidence for each dimension. Deliver the one-page summary first, followed by the detailed scorecard, assumptions, calculation and sources.

The letters supply the investment principles. The scoring weights, bands, and numerical valuation assumptions are conventions for this skill.
