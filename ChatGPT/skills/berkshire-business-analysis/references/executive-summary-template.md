# One-page executive summary

Keep the decision-maker view to one printable page, typically 300–400 words including compact labels. Use a dated title with company and ticker, a prominent numeric score and five visual stars (for example, 65/100 and ★★★☆☆, 3 of 5), and one sentence summarizing the investment case.

- **Business and durability** — Who pays, what makes the economics attractive, and which change could alter them.
- **Owner economics** — Two or three figures, including the cash-generation or return measure that matters most. Identify period and units.
- **Price and scenarios** — Dated price, downside/base/upside values, central margin of safety and the assumption that drives the range.
- **Scorecard** — Six weighted contributions and the total. A two-column or compact table works.
- **What changes the view** — Two observable events tied to the business judgment or valuation. State the principal evidence limitation.
- **Sources** — Short linked citations to central filings and regulatory material. Put the detailed model and full citations in the supporting analysis.

For the visual rating, render five symbols using filled and outlined stars. Include the text form for accessibility and retain it in exports. If the desired artifact is a PDF, confirm the final export is one page and the stars render.
