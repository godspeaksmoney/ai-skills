# Indian Energy Exchange Limited

Historical worked example using public sources.

**NSE IEX · BSE 540750 · 20 November 2026**

**65/100 · ★★★☆☆ · 3 of 5 · Mixed**

IEX has an asset-light, cash-generative exchange. Its valuation gives shareholders little room for an erosion of the trading advantage that supports its fees.

**Business and durability.** IEX charges participants to transact and access its platform. Transaction fees supplied 95% of FY26 operating revenue. Liquidity helps attract traders, and bidding and back-office integrations can give customers reasons to stay. CERC's April 2026 draft would pool bids and put clearing-price discovery with Grid India. That could let rivals compete for orders using the same clearing price. I found no final coupling notification by the review date. [FY26 annual report](https://doc.iexindia.com/files/annual-report-1uPuHcnt6Ccp.pdf#page=270) · [CERC draft](https://cercind.gov.in/2026/draft_reg/DN-PM-2ndAmd.pdf) · [Current regulations](https://cercind.gov.in/current_reg.html)

**Owner economics.** FY26 consolidated operating revenue was ₹616 crore, profit was ₹493 crore and operating cash flow less total capex was ₹418 crore. Q2 FY27 electricity volume rose about 13% year over year, and consolidated profit rose about 13% to ₹139 crore. Normalized trailing core owner earnings are estimated at ₹380 crore, excluding other income and equity-accounted profit. [FY26 annual report](https://doc.iexindia.com/files/annual-report-1uPuHcnt6Ccp.pdf#page=239) · [Q2 results](https://doc.iexindia.com/files/IEX-Standalone-and-Consolidated-Unaudited-Financial-Results-for-the-quarter-and-half-year-ended-September-30--2026-5CKS5nNqeOqT.pdf#page=10) · [Q2 presentation](https://doc.iexindia.com/files/Investor-Presentation-on-Unaudited-Financials-Result-for-the-quarter-ended-September-30--2026--TOfHRY5LOi5t.pdf)

**Price.** The 19 November BSE close was ₹115.20, or about 19.6× trailing reported earnings. The model gives ₹51 downside, ₹71 base and ₹94 upside per share. The base-case margin of safety is −62%. The range depends on which segments are coupled and how much volume and fee income IEX retains.
