# Shareholder letter intake

Follow the annual shareholder letters by Berkshire reporting year. The official sources are [Warren Buffett's letter index](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/letters.html), [Greg Abel's letter index](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/gealetters.html), and [Berkshire's annual reports](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/reports.html). The initial coverage is 1977–2025. The annual report can contain a new letter before the dedicated index is updated.

1. Compare the official indices and the latest annual report with [the manifest](letter-manifest.json). Count an annual letter once when the standalone file and annual report contain the same text. Confirm the reporting year, author, and exact URL from the source. A revised link or a changed document merits checking its identity or contents.
2. Read each new or substantively revised letter, including relevant footnotes, tables, and exhibits. Record original notes on the economic principle, business example and decision it changes, with a PDF page or HTML heading where useful. Preserve attribution and distinguish observations from later interpretations.
3. Add the verified source and the note together. Preserve prior years and editions, update the coverage range and thematic pointers in [letter notes](letter-notes.md), and revise the scoring guidance only when the new letter changes a method. Update the skill's description and coverage statements when the date range changes.
4. Check that every manifest year has exactly one note and its official link. Use publisher URLs in the notes and record partial coverage or an access failure for retry. A clean check with no new or revised letters requires no archive edit.

For new archive rows, preserve `year`, `url` and `format`. Record page count, low-text pages and source hash when they were verified. The original files are served by the publisher.
