## 124 · Size Matters: The Kelly Criterion and the Importance of Money Management

**Listed:** 2006 · Legg Mason · Michael J. Mauboussin · printed 2006-02-01  
**Source:** [12mv2.com](https://12mv2.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/06-02-01-size-matters__the-kelly-criterion-and-the-importance-of-money-management.pdf)

**Question.** How should investors size positions to maximise long-term wealth?

**Argument and evidence.**
- Kelly sizing sets the optimal bet as a fraction of bankroll tied to edge; overbetting risks ruin (p. 3).
- Geometric, not arithmetic, returns drive compounding (p. 6).
- Typical funds are widely diversified (p. 2).

**Application.** Size positions by edge and odds, as concentrated investors have done when conviction was high (p. 10).

**Limitations.** Requires persistent edges and long horizons; returns are non-normal (p. 6).
