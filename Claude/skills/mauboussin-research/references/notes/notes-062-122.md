## 062 · Measuring the Moat: Assessing the Magnitude and Sustainability of Value Creation

**Listed:** 2016 · Credit Suisse · Michael J. Mauboussin · printed 2016-11-01  
**Source:** [12mv2.com](https://12mv2.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/16-11-01-measuring-the-moat__assessing-the-magnitude-and-sustainability-of-value-creation.pdf)

**Question.** How large is a company's moat and how long can it sustain value creation?

**Argument and evidence.**
- Firm-specific strategy and resources explain most of the gap for below-average performers, while industry matters more for sustaining top performers (p. 10).
- Strong industries contain value destroyers and weak ones contain creators (p. 8).
- The period of sustained excess returns has been shrinking (p. 5).

**Application.** Analyse the industry (map, profit pool, stability, five forces) and then firm-specific added value to judge moat size and durability.

**Limitations.** Relies on proprietary data and older studies; largely qualitative. Text after about p. 35 not read.

**Editions.** Second edition, between 101 (2013) and 012 (2024).

- Persistence and predictiveness are distinct properties (p. 3).
