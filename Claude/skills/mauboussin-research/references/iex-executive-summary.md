# Indian Energy Exchange (NSE: IEX) — executive summary

**60/100 · ★★★☆☆** (plausible 58–63, ★★★☆☆–★★★★☆) · confidence medium · provisional on market-coupling rules  
Analysis date 2026-11-20 · price ₹115.17, NSE close of 19 Nov 2026 (corroborated by two or more timestamped sources) · historical example, not a current view

**Thesis.** IEX is an exceptional business today. It runs a nearly capital-free exchange with dominant liquidity and very high returns. But its moat depends on liquidity concentrated at one venue, and CERC's market-coupling proposal would pool bids from all exchanges and discover a single clearing price centrally. At ₹115.17 the market prices growth as if that risk were small. The probability-weighted value is ₹90.82, about 21% below the price.

**Decisive evidence**

- *Reported:* Q2 FY27 consolidated PAT ₹139 crore, up 12.9%. Electricity volume 39.7 BU, up 12.7%. H1 FY27 volume up 14.2%. FY26 volume 141 BU, up 17%.
- *Proposed rule:* the draft CERC (Power Market) (Second Amendment) Regulations of 17 Apr 2026 make Grid India the market-coupling operator for DAM and RTM, with start dates to be notified. The comment window closed 5 Jun 2026. The rule was not final as of the cutoff.
- *Enacted/legal:* APTEL (13 Feb 2026) held that coupling can proceed only after CERC frames regulations. CERC staff reviewed exchange transaction fees in Dec 2025.
- *Management view:* market share in DAM and RTM stays near 99%. Coupling is not expected this calendar year; the chairman suggested 1.5–2 years, as relayed in a broker note. A buyback is under consideration.
- *Model treatment:* client settlement balances are excluded and settlement working capital is normalized to zero. Treasury income and the IGX profit share are left out of core cash flow. Instead the bridge adds ₹1,000 crore of excess financial assets and a ₹500 crore IGX stake (both estimates).
